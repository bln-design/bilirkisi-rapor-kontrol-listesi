# Expert report checklist (Turkey)

Version 1.1.0 · 2026-10-23 · Abbreviations: HMK = Code of Civil Procedure (Hukuk Muhakemeleri Kanunu, Law No. 6100); CMK = Code of Criminal Procedure (Ceza Muhakemesi Kanunu, Law No. 5271); Law No. 6754 = Law on Expert Witnesses (Bilirkişilik Kanunu); Expert Witness Regulation = Bilirkişilik Yönetmeliği.

The items are general and do not cover methods specific to any field of expertise. References point to the relevant provision and do not replace its text.

Turkish version: [tr/KONTROL_LISTESI.md](tr/KONTROL_LISTESI.md)

## Appointment and time limit

- [ ] The appointing authority, case number and date of appointment have been checked.
- [ ] It has been assessed whether the question falls within the field of expertise; if it does not, or if cooperation with another expert is needed, the authority was notified within one week. (HMK art. 275/1; Expert Witness Regulation art. 53)
- [ ] Before starting the examination, it has been assessed whether there is any kinship, business relationship or other ground requiring recusal with respect to the parties. (HMK art. 272; Expert Witness Regulation art. 9/3, art. 53)
- [ ] The deadline for the assignment has been noted. (HMK art. 274/1; CMK art. 66/1; Expert Witness Regulation art. 52/1)
- [ ] If the time limit is insufficient, a reasoned request for extension was made before it expired. (HMK art. 274/1; CMK art. 66/1; Expert Witness Regulation art. 52/1)
- [ ] Where the scope or limits of the assignment were unclear, clarification was requested from the authority. (HMK art. 278/2; Expert Witness Regulation art. 54/2)

## Review of the case file

- [ ] The case file and annexes received have been compared against the index of documents; any missing items have been listed.
- [ ] Additional records and documents needed for the examination were requested from the authority within one week. (HMK art. 275/2; Expert Witness Regulation art. 54/1)
- [ ] The questions of the authority have been listed one by one, and the place in the report where each will be answered has been identified.
- [ ] The information and documents in the file have been kept confidential and not shared with third parties or with external services unrelated to the case. (Law No. 6754 art. 3/5; HMK art. 277)
- [ ] The examination was carried out in person; no part of the assignment was delegated to anyone else. (Law No. 6754 art. 3/4; HMK art. 276)

## Site inspection and examination

- [ ] Where an examination of an object is required, it has been confirmed that the authority has issued a decision for it. (HMK art. 278/4)
- [ ] Persons present during the site inspection or examination have been recorded.
- [ ] Findings have been recorded with place, date and time; photographs have been numbered.
- [ ] Where information is to be obtained from the parties, the approval of the authority was sought; one party was not heard in the absence of the other. (HMK art. 278/3)
- [ ] No conversation outside the scope of the file was held with the parties during the site inspection, and no opinion was expressed. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 8/2)

## Structure of the report

- [ ] The appointing authority, case number, party details, date of appointment and time limit have been stated. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 55/1-a, b, c, ç; HMK art. 279/2)
- [ ] The subject of the examination, the material elements to be examined and a list of the documents examined have been given. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 55/1-d, e)
- [ ] The method of examination and its scientific and technical basis have been stated. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 55/1-f, g)
- [ ] Each question has received a separate, reasoned answer; no question has been left unanswered. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 55/1-ğ; CMK art. 67/1)
- [ ] The conclusion has been read against the assessment section to confirm there is no contradiction between them.
- [ ] Where there is more than one expert, any dissenting opinion has been written with its reasons. (HMK art. 279/2; CMK art. 67/2; Expert Witness Regulation art. 55/2)
- [ ] The date of the report and the identity and signature details of the expert have been included. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 55/1-h, ı; HMK art. 279/2)
- [ ] The format has been compared with the report templates and guiding principles of the Expert Witness Department. (Expert Witness Department (Bilirkişilik Daire Başkanlığı), report templates and guiding principles)

## Language and limits

- [ ] The report has been confined to matters whose resolution requires expertise or special or technical knowledge. (Law No. 6754 art. 3/2; HMK art. 279/4; CMK art. 67/3)
- [ ] No legal characterisation or assessment reserved to the judge has been made. (Law No. 6754 art. 3/2; HMK art. 279/4; CMK art. 67/3; Expert Witness Regulation art. 55/4)
- [ ] The language is impartial; there is no wording that favours or disparages either party. (Law No. 6754 art. 3/1)
- [ ] Assumptions have been clearly identified as assumptions, and their basis has been stated.
- [ ] No expert opinion, consultancy or similar work was accepted from the parties during the assignment. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 9/2)
- [ ] Any unethical or unlawful request received has been reported to the authority. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 13)

## Annexes and signature

- [ ] Annexes such as diagrams, sketches, photographs and tables have been numbered, referred to in the text, and match the list of annexes exactly. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 55/3)
- [ ] Personal data has been included only to the extent required by the purpose of the report.
- [ ] The report has been signed with a secure electronic signature. (Expert Witness Regulation art. 85/5)

## Submission via UYAP

- [ ] The report has been uploaded to the correct file and the correct authority through the UYAP Expert Portal (UYAP Bilirkişi Portal). (Expert Witness Regulation art. 85/5)
- [ ] The uploaded file has been opened to confirm it is the final version and the annexes are complete.
- [ ] The submission date is within the time limit given. (HMK art. 274/1; Expert Witness Regulation art. 52/1)
- [ ] Documents and items received for examination have been returned with an index of documents. (HMK art. 280/1)

## After submission

- [ ] If a supplementary report or clarification is requested, each point found incomplete or unclear has been addressed separately. (HMK art. 281/2)
- [ ] Confidentiality has been maintained after the assignment ended; retention of copies of the file has been arranged accordingly. (Law No. 6754 art. 3/5)
- [ ] Expense and fee documents have been submitted in the form requested by the authority. (HMK art. 283; CMK art. 72; Expert Witness Regulation art. 57)
